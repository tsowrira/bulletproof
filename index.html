// How it runs

Supersets. Do 1 → rest → 2 → rest, twice through. Tap ○ after round one (½), again after round two (✓) — each tap starts your rest.

Two sets. Set 1 leaves 2 in the tank. Set 2 = every clean rep you've got — type it in, beat it next week. Spine lifts stay fixed-rep. Machine set 2 hits ~15 clean → up one pin.

Short on time or energy? Express hides the bonus round. Tired → drop a set, keep the load. Wiped, or anything new down a leg → rest day.

S1 / S2 = set 1 / 2 · AMRAP−1 = every clean rep, stop one short · C2B = chest to bar · 3 · 1 · 2 = seconds down · pause · up (X = fast)

SKILLS & SWAPS

Skills you level up, accessories you swap when stale. The lifts you track stay put.

Skill ladders
Pull · Day A

Muscle-up: 10 strict pull-ups (done) → 12–15 + chest-to-bar × 5 → high pulls to the lower ribs → feet-assisted low-bar transitions (two feet → one → heels) → jump muscle-up with 5s lowering → strict muscle-up. Shoulders quiet for 48 h before each new rung; no kipping.

One-arm pull-up: strict → weighted (+10–25 lb) → archer → typewriter → one-arm negatives (5s, spare hand light on the wrist) → finger-assisted → one-arm pull-up. Soft elbow at the bottom. Measured in years.

Front lever: scap pulls (you’re here) → tuck hold → advanced tuck → single-leg → straddle. Posterior tilt, scaps packed; stop when the low back sags.

Pull variations: chin-up → wide-grip → close/commando → mixed-grip → around-the-worlds. L-sit pull-up: not yet — it squeezes the low back.

Push & balance · Day B

Handstand: back-to-wall hold → chest-to-wall hold → chest-to-wall shoulder taps → toe-pull floats (toes drift off the wall, 5 × 3–5s) → 1–2s free balance → 5s → 10s → shapes (tuck, straddle, stag). Wall is always the catch; active shoulders, never locked joints.

Handstand push-up: pike push-up → feet-elevated pike → 30s chest-to-wall hold → wall negatives (5s down) → partial → full wall HSPU. Wall only. Cap the depth — never sink into the shoulder.

Press to handstand: seated pike-compression lifts → wall tuck-ups → straddle-press negatives from the wall handstand → straddle press. Straight arms, hollow body, zero spinal load.

Planche line: frog stand 30s → crow / straight-arm frog → planche lean (soft elbow, shoulders past wrists) → tuck planche. Stay low, safe to bail. Past tuck planche: not yet — too much straight-arm load on elbows and shoulders.

Elbow lever: hands flat, elbows tucked into the lower ribs → tip forward → lever out flat. Low to the floor; grooves the straight-body brace.

Core & levers

Human flag: flagpole grip + support press → vertical hold → chamber/tuck flag 10–20s each side → single-leg → straddle → full. Rigid body, bottom shoulder packed. Tuck now; full flag not yet — same straight-arm load.

L-sit / compression: seated pike lifts → parallette support hold → tuck support with a hard posterior tilt → one leg out. Full L-sit: not yet — it squeezes the low back. Tucked posterior-tilt holds are the on-ramp.

Legs · Day C

Pistol: tall box sit → lower box → step-platform heel-tap (fingertips for balance only) → no-contact off the step. Off a step, never a wall; cap at parallel.

Cossack squat: wide stance → sink to one side, other leg straight, heel down → flow side to side (light plate at the chest to counterbalance). Control the descent — never a passive drop into the hip.

Shrimp squat: hold the rear foot → lower the rear knee to a pad → stand up. Gentler on the kneecap than a deep pistol.

Skater squat: shrimp without holding the foot → arms free to counterbalance → rear knee taps a pad. Shrimp’s balance challenge, one notch up.

Rule: clean for 3 reps/holds across 2 sessions → next rung. Earned by clean reps, not by the calendar.

// Off days · what's next · not yet

GAME PLAN

A plan for the off days, what earns your next skills, and what's waiting its turn.

Back or knee grumbling — the plan · decided now, not mid-grumble

Mild — annoying, nothing new down a leg. 3 days of Daily Low-Load + 20-min walks. Retest on day 4. Back to normal? Resume at half volume and rebuild over 3 sessions.

Sharper — interrupting sleep or daily stuff. Stop training; walk + easy hangs only. Still loud after 48 h → get it looked at.

Anything new down a leg — numbness, weakness — get it checked before training again. Numb saddle area or any bladder change: emergency care, same day.

What unlocks what · not yet ≠ never

Heavier hinge / trap bar from blocks: dumbbell RDL 25 lb 2 × 10 clean · side plank 3 × 40s each side · back-extension 12 × 10s clean · six steady months.

Freestanding handstand: 60s chest-to-wall hold · 10s chest-to-wall shoulder taps each side · wrists happy across 8 skill sessions in a row.

Unassisted pistol — ALL of: heel-tap pistol 3×6, no fingertips · L = R eyes-closed balance 20s · Bulgarian split squat 15 lb 2×8, knees quiet · single-leg eccentric step-down from 6" box 3×8, no knee cave-in · heel-tap pistol 3×6 at parallel + 1" with counterbalance.

Jump muscle-up, then strict — ALL of: 12–15 strict pull-ups · 5 chest-to-bar with sternum contact · 3 × 3 high pulls to the lower ribs · clean heels-only transitions · bar dips 2×10 at the 90° cap with 2s pauses. Every rung below that is open now.

Not yet — and why · one reason each
Single-leg RDL: lopsided hinge under load. Own the two-leg dumbbell RDL first.
Barbell deadlift from the floor: not yet. Trap bar from blocks comes first — see above.

Reverse Nordic: heavy on the front of the knee. Later: partial range, ramped slowly.
Passive static stretching: more range without control means wobblier joints, not better ones.
Dragon flag: arches the low back hard the instant the brace slips.
German hang / skin-the-cat: cranks the front of the shoulder at the end of its range.
Back lever · full bridge / wheel: deep low-back arch under load, and it cranks the front of the shoulder.
Jefferson curl: rounds the low back under load through the full range.
Hanging leg raise · toes-to-bar · full L-sit: squeezes the low back. Tuck versions only for now.
Ring dips · deep dips · strict transition: bar dips cap at 90°; the rest cranks the front of the shoulder.
