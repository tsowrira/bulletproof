// How it runs

Supersets. Do 1 → rest → 2 → rest, twice through. Tap ○ after every set: it logs that set (fix the numbers first if they changed), starts rest and shows the next set — S2, S3 — then ✓ after the last. Ramp sets (⚡ on the card) are not logged.

New lift? Ramp up, then pick the weight where set 1 hits the low end of its range with about 2 left. 4 or more left: up a pin, rest, redo it. None left: down a pin. Log what you ended on — that is your start. Fixed lifts start light and stay at the number on the card.

Two sets. Set 1 leaves 2 in the tank. Set 2 stops 1 short on the big lifts — never the last grind rep. On cable and machine isolation it is every clean rep unless the card says stop short. Log it on the big lifts. Same as last time is a good day. A card with a rep range: set 1 tops the range clean with 2 left → one pin or the next dumbbell next time. Other lifts: set 2 hits 15 clean → one pin up. Set 1 leaving 4 or more in the tank two sessions running → one pin up too. Reps drop a few after a jump, rebuild from there. Clean = tempo held, no swing.

Every 4 weeks tap Block — the accessories swap to a sibling lift, your main lifts and numbers stay.

One rule everywhere. Stop a set on joint pain, never on muscle burn.

How long? Pick one at the top of the day. 20 Tired: first drill + two lifts, every set stops 3 short. 45 Built (default): picked from your logs — lifts you keep missing go first, a skipped day's key lifts come along. 60 Full: everything on the page. 80 Long: every drill, a third set on the key lifts. Skip freely: never make up, never double up.

S1/S2 = set 1/2 · AMRAP = all clean reps (−1/−2 = stop that many short) · C2B = chest to bar · fixed = no extra reps · ISO = hold · 3 · 1 · 2 = secs lower · pause · lift · X = fast

// Off days · what's next · not yet · left vs right

GAME PLAN

A plan for the off days, what earns your next skills, what's waiting, and a monthly left–right check.

Back or knee grumbling — the plan decided now, not mid-grumble

Mild — annoying, nothing new down a leg. 3 days of Daily Low-Load + 20-min walks. Retest on day 4. Back to normal? Resume at half volume and rebuild over 3 sessions.

Sharper — interrupting sleep or daily stuff. Stop training; walk + easy active hangs only. Still loud after 48 h → get it looked at.

Anything new down a leg — numbness, weakness: get it checked before training. Numbness between the legs or bladder changes: get help the same day.

What unlocks what not yet ≠ never

Heavier hinge: dumbbell RDL 25 lb 2 × 10 clean · side plank 2 × 40s each · back-extension 12 × 10s clean · six steady months → RDL past 35 lb, or a cable pull-through.

Handstand shapes (tuck, straddle): freestanding 20s on 3 of 6 kick-ups · shoulder taps 3 × 4 each at the wall, hips square · wrists happy across 8 skill sessions in a row.

Unassisted pistol — ALL of: heel-tap pistol 2 × 6 to parallel, no fingertips · forward step-down 2 × 15 clean, no knee cave-in · split squat 25 lb 2 × 8, knees quiet · L = R eyes-closed balance 20s.

Toes-on-box muscle-up, then strict — ALL of: 12–15 strict pull-ups · 5 chest-to-bar with sternum contact · 3 × 3 high pulls to the lower ribs · clean heels-only transitions · bar dips 2 × 10 at the 90° cap with 2s pauses. Every rung below that is open now.

Not yet — and why one reason each
Single-leg RDL: lopsided hinge under load. Own the two-leg dumbbell RDL first.
Barbell deadlift from the floor: not yet. The heavier hinge comes first — see above.
Reverse hyperextension: swings the low back into a deep arch. The active hang covers it.
Hollow body hold: rounds the low back under load. Pallof and dead bug cover the core.
Curtsy lunge: twists the knee and low back together. Hip thrust and the abductor machine cover it.
Reverse Nordic: heavy on the front of the knee. Later: partial range, ramped slowly.
Passive static stretching: more range without control means wobblier joints, not better ones.
Dragon flag: arches the low back hard the instant the brace slips.
German hang / skin-the-cat: cranks the front of the shoulder at the end of its range.
Back lever · full bridge / wheel: deep low-back arch under load, and it cranks the front of the shoulder.
Jefferson curl: rounds the low back under load through the full range.
Hanging leg raise · toes-to-bar · full L-sit: squeezes the low back. Pelvic-tuck hangs only for now.
Ring dips · deep dips · strict transition: bar dips cap at 90°; the rest cranks the front of the shoulder.
Full planche · human flag: max straight-arm load on elbows and shoulders. Planche leans are the on-ramp.
Left vs right — 5-minute check once a month

1. Rear photo relaxed, then glutes squeezed, then standing on each leg — look for a size gap or the free hip dropping.

2. Single-leg bridge, heel on a chair, to clean failure each side, right first — aim 25+, sides within 10%.

3. Rear video: 5 slow arm raises forward and 5 to the side with 2–3 lb. Flag a shoulder-blade edge lifting or one side shrugging early.

4. Side photo in your normal barefoot stance — knees should sit at straight, not pushed back.

5. Front video: slow step-down off an 8″ step × 5 each, right first — flag the knee drifting inside the second toe.

6. Every three months: longest clean back-extension hold, side plank each side, single-leg bridge each side, comfortable brisk-walk time. A drop of a fifth, or a growing left–right gap: get it looked at.

A side 10% behind gets one extra set until it catches up.
